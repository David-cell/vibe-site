Vibe Coding Tools ("we", "us", "the site") provides free, browser-based developer and utility tools. This policy explains what data we handle — and, importantly, what we do not handle.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

All of our tools (the AI cost calculator, the prompt builder, the model compare, and the CLAUDE.md generator) run entirely inside your web browser. The tokens, prompts, project details, and rule files you enter are never uploaded to our servers. We do not have a backend, database, or API that receives your input. We cannot see, log, or store your data.

2. Local Storage

We do not use local storage or cookies of our own to track you or persist your inputs. Any temporary data held by the browser during a tool's operation is discarded when you leave the page.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Cookies

To keep the tools free, this site may display advertisements served by third-party networks such as Google AdSense. These partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. This is standard behavior for ad-supported sites and is governed by the third party's own privacy policies.

  • You can review how Google uses advertising cookies here: Google Ads Policy.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings or YourAdChoices.

4. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date above.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or the site, contact us at hello@david-cells.com.

Bottom line: the tools themselves collect nothing. The only tracking on this site comes from third-party ad networks, which you can opt out of at any time.
