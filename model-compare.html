Frequently asked questions

How do I pick the right model?
Start from your constraint: huge context — Gemini 3.1 Pro or Grok 4.3 (2M); cheapest volume — DeepSeek V4 Flash or Gemini 3.5 Flash; best coding — Claude Sonnet 5 or GPT-5.5. Use the filter to narrow, then verify pricing on the provider site.
Why do input and output prices differ?
Generating tokens (output) costs more compute than reading them (input), so nearly every provider charges more per output token.
Are these prices current?
They are indicative snapshots. AI pricing moves fast and often includes cached-token discounts, so confirm before budgeting.
Is the comparison data uploaded?
No. Filtering happens entirely in your browser.
