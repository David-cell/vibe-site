Frequently asked questions

Why do I need a brief if I have a prompt builder?
A prompt tells the agent what to build this turn. A brief captures the product: who it is for, what it must and must not do. You stay aligned across many turns instead of re-explaining the idea each time.
Is my idea uploaded?
No. The brief is assembled in your browser from your inputs. Nothing leaves your device.
What do I do with the output?
Paste it into a CLAUDE.md, a Cursor project rule, or the first message of a new chat. It works as both a spec and a guardrail.
