Contact

Questions, feedback, bug reports, or a tool you wish existed? Reach out directly.

Email

The fastest way to reach us is by email:

hello@david-cells.com

We read every message and typically reply within a few days. Because this is a one-person project, please be patient if a reply takes a little longer during busy periods.

What to include

  • Bug reports — tell us which tool, what you did, and what you expected vs. what happened.
  • Tool requests — if you need a utility we do not have yet, we want to hear about it.
  • Privacy questions — see the Privacy Policy first; email us if anything is unclear.

A note on this site

Vibe Coding Tools is a static, client-side site. There is no contact form by design — submitting data through a server would contradict the privacy-first principle the project is built on. A direct email link keeps things simple and keeps your message in your hands.

Prefer to browse first? The Blog covers how every tool works, and the About page explains the project's philosophy.
